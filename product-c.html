C Series

C-Series printing squeegee features high wear resistance, excellent solvent tolerance, and good elasticity to ensure optimal printing results. Its balanced performance offers a cost-effective solution, making it both versatile and economical for various applications.

Key Features

High Wear Resistance

Features high abrasion resistance and excellent tolerance to solvents, supporting reliable performance over extended printing runs.

Good Elasticity

Superior resilience guarantees optimal printing results and helps the squeegee maintain consistent contact during production.

Balanced Performance

Delivers a high cost-performance ratio, making it a more economical and versatile choice for various printing applications.
