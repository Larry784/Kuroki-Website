About KUROKI

Dedicated to the R&D and manufacturing of high-performance polyurethane elastomer products for over 30 years.

Our Story

KUROKI Co., Ltd. is dedicated to the R&D and manufacturing of polyurethane (PU) elastomer products. With over 30 years of rich manufacturing experience, we have earned the trust of many internationally renowned enterprises across various industries.

From printing consumables and screen printing to mining equipment and construction machinery, we have become one of the largest polyurethane product manufacturers in Taiwan and Mainland China.

Expansion & Growth

To better respond to market demands and serve customers, starting in 2000, the company successively established factories and branches in Xiamen, Shanghai, Suzhou, Shenzhen, Urumqi, and other locations across China, forming a corporate group structure.

We aim to co-create a brighter future through solid technical strength, production capacity, and cost efficiency.
