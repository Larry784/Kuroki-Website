Solvent-Resistant Squeegees

X/XP (TW) Series

A classic and well-received Kuroki model since it was launched in 2008. Its outstanding solvent-resistant property makes it suitable for various high precise printing tasks.

Key Features

Solvent & Wear Resistance

Exceptional Solvent Resistance and Wear Resistance: Serves as a full replacement for high-end imported printing squeegees, significantly optimizing process costs.

Re-sharpenable

Can be resharpened multiple times to increase reuse cycles and maximize cost efficiency.

Ultra-Low Deformation

Highly resistant to solvent swelling with minimal deformation during use, making it ideal for high-precision screen printing while offering superior protection to the printing screen.

Application Scenario

Suitable for glass printing, touch panel (TP) printing, toys, children's products, and other high-precision printing environments.
