Solvent-Resistant Squeegees

SR Series

SR utilizes a larger-scale molding process suitable for subsequent processing and shaping into various blade shapes and lengths, providing a more economical and versatile choice for solvent-resistant squeegees.

Key Features

Solvent & Wear Resistance

Extends press run times per setup, reduces downtime for blade replacements, and optimizes process costs.

Re-sharpenable

Can be resharpened multiple times to maximize cost efficiency and usability.

Low Deformation

Low deformation and excellent wear resistance make SR widely used in high-precision electronics screen printing, providing maximum protection for the printing mesh and screen.
