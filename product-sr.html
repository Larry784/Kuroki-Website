Solvent-Resistant Squeegees

S/SV(TW) Series

S-Series utilizes a larger-scale molding process suitable for subsequent processing and shaping into various blade shapes and lengths, providing a more economical and versatile choice for solvent-resistant squeegees.

Key Features

Solvent & Wear Resistance

Extends press run times per setup, reduces downtime for blade replacements, and optimizes process costs.

Re-sharpenable

Can be resharpened multiple times to maximize cost efficiency and usability.

Low Deformation

Low deformation and excellent wear resistance make S widely used in high-precision electronics screen printing, providing maximum protection for the printing mesh and screen.

Application Scenario

Designed for the electronics industry, including printed circuit boards (PCBs), passive components, solar cells, and nameplates/signage.
