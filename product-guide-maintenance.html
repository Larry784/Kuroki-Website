Maintenance Guidelines

Follow these maintenance practices to preserve squeegee performance, extend service life, and maintain consistent printing quality.

Protect Performance Through Every Production Cycle

Proper handling before, during, and after use helps protect the blade from premature deformation, chemical damage, and unnecessary wear.

Service Life

  • Squeegee lifespan depends on factors such as operating pressure, speed, ink chemistry, and screen mesh type.
  • Ink solvents penetrate the squeegee material, causing swelling and changes in physical properties. Alternating between two sets of squeegees or rotating a single blade's orientation allows the blade to rest and recover, significantly extending its overall service life.
Caution

Do not wait until pressure and solvents cause permanent deformation before replacing the blade.

Cleaning

  • After removal, wipe off excess solvent and ink from the blade using cardboard or a cotton cloth before washing with an appropriate solvent or cleaning system.
Caution

Avoid using corrosive or highly polar solvents for cleaning.

Resharpening

  • Before regrinding the squeegee edge, allow it to rest for at least 12 to 24 hours so absorbed solvents can fully evaporate.
  • Due to the material's excellent grindability, prevent excessive heat generation during grinding by reducing pressure and shortening processing time.

Storage

  • For medium- to long-term storage, lay the squeegees flat to prevent warping or curling. Keep the storage environment dry and protected from direct sunlight.
  • Prolonged storage in cold environments may cause blade hardness to increase. If this occurs, bake the squeegee in an oven at 30°C–40°C for 30 to 60 minutes. Once it cools back down to room temperature, the hardness will return to its original factory level.

Maintenance Priorities

A consistent maintenance routine protects both the squeegee and the printing process.

Rest Before Resharpening

Allow absorbed solvents to evaporate before grinding so the blade can be processed under more stable conditions.

Store Flat and Dry

Prevent warping, curling, and unnecessary hardness changes by controlling the storage position and environment.
